# Project Meaning

## Working Name

RecruitBuddy / JobHunter AI.

The product is a job-search portal with an AI recruiter. It helps a job seeker treat job hunting like focused work instead of a pile of tabs, documents, deadlines, and repeated one-off applications.

## Core Idea

Job searching should feel like running a clear project:

- one profile that explains who the user is;
- one structured CV that acts as the factual source of truth;
- one place for job sources such as Finn, Arbeidsplassen, LinkedIn, RSS feeds, pasted URLs, and manual job text;
- one pipeline for discovered jobs, drafts, sent applications, responses, interviews, offers, rejections, and follow-ups;
- one calendar for deadlines, milestones, interviews, and reminders;
- one AI recruiter that helps prioritize, write, tailor, and keep momentum.

Today, job seekers often handle each application separately. A job ad lives in one tab, a CV version in another folder, a cover letter in a document, deadlines in memory, and follow-ups nowhere. This app should gather the whole process into a single operating surface.

## Product Promise

Jobbsoking gjort enkelt.

The app should help the user answer:

- What should I do today?
- Which jobs are worth my attention?
- What is the status of every application?
- What deadlines or follow-ups are coming?
- What should my CV and application emphasize for this role?
- Am I making progress toward getting a job?

## Product Metaphor

The job search is a job.

The dashboard is the daily workbench. Sources bring in opportunities. The pipeline keeps track of status. The calendar protects deadlines. The AI recruiter helps with judgment and execution.

## Target Users

Primary user:

- actively looking for a job;
- applying to multiple roles at once;
- wants to stay organized;
- wants AI help but still wants control;
- needs honest tailoring, not fabricated claims.

Likely expansion users:

- career changers;
- students and recent graduates;
- laid-off workers;
- consultants or contractors;
- people applying across several platforms.

## Core Objects

- **Profile**: the user's master story, preferences, scoring weights, green/yellow/red rules, and writing style.
- **CV Template**: structured factual career data. This is the evidence base. AI must not invent outside it.
- **Job**: a discovered opportunity, parsed and scored against the profile.
- **Application**: a draft or sent cover letter tied to a job.
- **CV Tweak**: AI guidance for tailoring the CV to a specific application.
- **Source**: where jobs come from, such as manual input, URL, RSS, Finn, Arbeidsplassen, LinkedIn, or file import.
- **Goal**: a target or milestone for job-search progress.
- **Calendar Event**: interview, follow-up, deadline, note, or custom event.

## Main Loop

1. User configures profile and CV.
2. User connects or adds job sources.
3. App discovers jobs and AI parses/scores them.
4. User triages jobs and marks interest.
5. AI generates a draft application and suggests CV tailoring.
6. User edits, exports, sends, and marks status.
7. Dashboard and calendar surface next actions.
8. Progress and feedback improve the next cycle.

## What This Is Not

This is not only a cover-letter generator.

It should not become a generic CRM, generic calendar, or generic AI chat app. Every feature should serve the user's job-search momentum.

It should not encourage spam applications. The better product is fewer, clearer, better-prioritized applications with less chaos.

## Success Criteria

The user should feel:

- less scattered;
- more in control;
- clearer about priorities;
- faster at producing good drafts;
- less likely to miss deadlines or follow-ups;
- able to keep applying even when the process is emotionally heavy.

The product succeeds when the user opens the app and knows what to do next.
